## Classification

| Scenario | Title | Mechanism | Local proof possible | Cluster today | What blocks the cluster |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `baseline-order` | Make it so | **Service API** (order create; reservation and fulfillment follow by events). ERP shipping is a **guided console step** (default mode is MANUAL; ship, or switch to AUTO) | Yes: services in containers + ERP harness | **Partly.** Order → reservation possible once P-1 is solved; "ships and completes" is **not yet possible** | P-1 (site/SKU rows), P-3 (no ERP), P-6 (chain unproven) |
| `inventory-contention` | The Trouble with Tribbles | **Service API**: two concurrent order creates; inventory's conditional update serialises them, the loser gets `inventory.rejected.v1` `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` and its order becomes `REJECTED` | Yes | **Not yet possible** | P-1 (no `site_ds9`/`CAT-TRIBBLE-TOY` row); reset to exactly one available needs P-2 (restock/release role) |
| `broker-outage` | Subspace interference | **Platform-level.** No bounded mechanism exists. Options: client-side egress NetworkPolicy in each service namespace, or stopping the Kafka broker (single KRaft node; Argo does not auto-sync Kafka, so a live change would persist until reverted) | Yes (pause a local Kafka container) | **Not yet possible** | No approved mechanism or RBAC; affects every service on the shared broker |
| `erp-manual-hold` | Hold that order, Number One | **Guided ERP console step** (`H` hold, `L` release); no fault flag needed | Yes: harness + existing console driver `tests/test_tui.py` | **Not yet possible to verify** | P-3: the hold cannot reach or be observed through the adapter |
| `erp-delayed-processing` | Impulse power only | **Guided ERP console step** (`X`, `3`, delay seconds); needs `faults = true` | Yes | **Not yet possible** | P-3, P-4 |
| `erp-crash-after-commit` | Warp core breach | **Guided ERP console step** (`X`, `2`, confirm); one-shot, disarms itself before the crash; needs `faults = true` | Yes | **Not yet possible** | P-3, P-4 |
| `erp-unavailable` | The ERP has cloaked | **VM-level**: stop the `legacy-erp` VM | Partly (stop the harness container) | **Not yet possible** | No RBAC for `virtualmachines/stop`; Argo `selfHeal` restores `runStrategy: Always`; P-3 means nothing would observe the outage |
| `poison-event` | Q was here | **Platform-level**: no service publishes raw events, so the only route is the controller producing a malformed record directly to a business topic. | `bad-artifact-gate` | Unsigned shuttlecraft denied docking | **Not yet possible as written**: nothing checks signatures or trust. A **guided** variant exists: an operator opens a workloads PR pointing at an outside image and `box1-gitops-validate` fails it; the controller reads the public check result | Guided variant only | **Not yet possible** (signature refusal); guided variant needs a decision | P-8 |
| `red-alert` | Red alert | **Service API** (asset-service `/api/v1/control/*`, including `stop-all`) | Yes, against a local asset-service with a test token, but this would not prove a token the deployed service accepts | **Not yet possible** | P-5 needs an asset-service change (out of scope, stop); chaos also needs the D-015 allowlist |

Summary: on the cluster today, **no scenario can run end to end.** With P-1 and P-2 solved, `inventory-contention` and the reservation half of `baseline-order` become possible. Every ERP scenario needs P-3, and three need P-4 as well.
